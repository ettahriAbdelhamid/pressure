In this thesis, we prepare all the tools needed to do a high-precision measurement of the pressure in a Quark-Gluon plasma using Lattice Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) for $N_f=2+1$ using the tree level symanzik improved gauge action and the HISQ fermion action. We first introduce the continuum quantum field theory and how Lagrangian naturally arises from the free Dirac Lagrangian by enforcing $SU(3)$ gauge invariance. Afterwards, we look at the implementation of QCD on the lattice. This section is followed by a short review of statistical mechanics and the introduction of the integral method which will be used to achieve the precision measurement of the pressure. Then, we will look at Monte Carlo integration which will be used to do lattice measurements. Finally, we will end the thesis with an outlook of the planned lattice measurements.
